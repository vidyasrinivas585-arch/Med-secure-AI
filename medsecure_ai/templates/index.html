What We Detect

Complete Protection Suite

Six powerful AI modules working together to verify every aspect of medicine authenticity.

Visual AI Detection
MobileNetV2 transfer learning classifies medicine packaging as Genuine or Counterfeit with 94.3% accuracy using deep visual pattern recognition.
Bilingual OCR
EasyOCR reads medicine name, manufacturer, batch number, and expiry date in both English and Kannada script automatically.
QR & Barcode Scanner
Scan QR codes and barcodes from uploaded images or live camera. Supports JSON, URL, GS1, and pipe-separated formats.
Expiry Detection
Automatically extracts and validates expiry dates, calculates days remaining, and flags expired medicines as high risk.
Authenticity Score
Combines AI confidence, OCR confidence, and packaging quality into a weighted 0–100 score with Low / Medium / High risk classification.
PDF Reports
Generate and download detailed PDF analysis reports with medicine image, all scores, OCR results, and risk recommendation.
